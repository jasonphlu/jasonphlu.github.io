Jason Lu

Hello! I am a Civil Engineering PhD Candidate at the University of Michigan, where I am advised by Prof. Neda Masoud in the Next Generation Mobility Systems Lab. I am also pursuing my M.S. in Industrial and Operations Engineering. My research focuses on optimization and machine learning methodologies for transportation systems. For the 2024-2025 academic year, I was the Vice President of the Michigan Transportation Student Organization.

I obtained my B.S. in Industrial Engineering with a Minor in Scientific and Engineering Computing at Georgia Tech, where I was an Undergraduate Research Assistant in the Socially Aware Mobility Lab and the Artificial Intelligence Institute for Advances in Optimization under Prof. Pascal Van Hentenryck. I was also the instructor of the The Seth Bonder Camp in Computational and Data Science for Engineering.

In my free time, you can find me eating Korean BBQ, traveling, going to the movies, or watching NASCAR and UFC.
